Privacy Policy for Tap Benteng

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Novaldy Paringgi (operating as Armacianore Studio) built the Tap Benteng mobile game as a free-to-play / freemium application. This page informs players regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using our game.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not require you to create an account or directly provide personally identifiable information (such as your name, physical address, or phone number) to play Tap Benteng.

  • Local Device Storage: Game settings, unlocked items, and local progress are stored locally on your Android device.
  • Local Network & Multiplayer (Unity Netcode for GameObjects): Tap Benteng supports local Wi-Fi / LAN multiplayer using Unity Netcode for GameObjects. During local multiplayer sessions, temporary network data (such as local IP addresses and gameplay state packets) is transmitted over your local network strictly to synchronize gameplay between connected devices and is not stored on external servers by us.
  • Engine & Platform Diagnostics: The game is built with Unity and distributed via Google Play, which may automatically process basic, non-personally identifiable technical information (such as device model, Android OS version, and crash logs) to ensure stability and compatibility.

2. Third-Party Services

Tap Benteng uses trusted platform services provided by Unity and Google Play that may collect information used to identify your device for core functionality, distribution, and future optional in-app purchases:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing
  • Unity Technologies Privacy Policy

3. In-App Purchases & Future Features

Future updates to Tap Benteng may offer optional in-app purchases (such as virtual gems or cosmetic items) processed securely through Google Play Billing, optional Google Play Games sign-in, or in-game promotional banners. We do not collect or store your payment card or financial details; all transactions are handled directly by Google Play. If advertising or third-party SDKs are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy and our Google Play Data Safety disclosures will be updated accordingly.

4. Data Security

We value your trust and strive to use commercially acceptable practices to protect any technical data processed during gameplay. However, no method of electronic transmission or local storage is 100% secure.

5. Children's Privacy

Tap Benteng is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as new features are added to the game. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
